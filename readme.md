# Dracula PRO

Welcome to Dracula PRO. We are the **Dracula Team**, and we are here to help you _become more productive._

## 1. Choosing the right theme

As a creator, you will spend a lot of time in your tools. A matching color scheme across your apps helps you stay focused and switch contexts faster.

1. Go to the `themes` folder.
2. Pick your favorite app folder first - you can set up the others later.
3. Check the `install.md` for instructions on how to set it up.

## 2. Picking the right font

You type letters for a living, so your font should feel natural and stay easy on the eyes.

1. Go to the `fonts` folder.
2. Check the `install.md` and install 1 font family you like before testing more.

## 3. Standardizing your environment

A unified setup across your apps helps you switch contexts with less friction.

1. Go to the `wallpapers` folder.
2. Configure a new wallpaper for your desktop, phone, and watch - start with desktop if you want a quick win.

You can also change the desktop icons to match your new wallpaper.

1. Go to the `icons` folder.
2. Pick your favorite app icons.
3. Check the `install.md` for instructions on how to set it up.

## 4. Adopting the right habits

You can have the best theme, font, and tooling in the world, but bad habits will still slow you down. Because of that, we asked top creators we know for practical tips to be more productive.

1. Go to the `content/book` folder.
2. Open the `.epub` or `.mobi` file in your favorite e-book app.

If you prefer listening over reading, check this out:

1. Go to the `content/audiobook` folder.
2. Pick the language you want to listen to.

Enjoy it, and tell us what you think about the book.
